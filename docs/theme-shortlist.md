## Evaluation priorities

1. Clear character separation for astigmatism and viewing from farther away
2. Strong but comfortable contrast between semantic colours
3. Avoid excessive saturation and glowing colours
4. Enough personality to feel designed rather than generic
5. Preserve readable logs and long terminal sessions
